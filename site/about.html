How a box reaches you

Our website is the shop window. Behind it, an ERP system handles purchasing from suppliers, stock levels and accounting, so the items you see are items we actually have.

  1. We buy from suppliersPurchase orders are raised in our ERP when stock runs low.
  2. Stock is received and countedGoods receipts update the quantity on hand that the website shows.
  3. Your box is packedItems are issued from stock, wrapped and handed to delivery.
