Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a signature box and a Build-Your-Own box?

A signature box is a ready-made set of items chosen by our team for one mood. It costs 10% less than buying the same items separately. A Build-Your-Own box lets you choose 3 to 8 items from any mood. You pay the item prices plus AED 15 for the gift box and wrapping.

Can I change the items in a signature box?

Yes. Open the box and choose Customise this box. Its items are copied into the box builder, where you can add, remove or swap anything. A customised box is priced as a Build-Your-Own box.

Why can't I add an item to my box?

Either the item is sold out, you already have 3 of that item, or your box already has 8 different items. Stock is checked again when you place your order, so you will never be charged for something we don't have.

Can I include a gift message?

Yes. Add a message of up to 200 characters in the box builder or in your cart. We print it on a card placed inside the lid. Prices are never printed in the box.

Which payment methods do you accept?

None at the moment: this website is a college demonstration and does not take payments. If BLOOMBOX launched as a real business, it would accept cards through a certified payment provider and cash on delivery.

Do you deliver outside the UAE?

Not yet. We deliver to all seven emirates.

How do you use my personal information?

We use your name, contact details and address only to process your order and answer your messages. They are visible only to store administrators. Our website analytics do not use cookies and do not record your IP address or name.

How to order

  1. Choose a signature box from the shop, or open the box builder and add 3 to 8 items.
  2. Add a gift message if you like, then add the box to your cart.
  3. In your cart, check quantities and the total. Delivery is free over AED 200.
  4. Enter your name, contact details and delivery address, then place your order.
  5. You'll see your order reference straight away (for example MB-261122-7K3QD). Keep it for any questions.

Delivery

  • Abu Dhabi and Dubai: 1–2 working days.
  • Other emirates: 2–4 working days.
  • Fees: free on orders of AED 200 or more; AED 15 otherwise.
  • Orders placed after 2 pm or on Saturday are packed the next working day.
  • Our courier contacts you by phone before delivery. If you're not available, they try again the next working day.

Returns policy and service guarantee

We want every box to arrive in perfect condition.

  • 7-day returns: return any unused item in its original packaging within 7 days of delivery for a full refund of that item's price.
  • Damaged or wrong items: tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace the item free of charge, including delivery.
  • Not returnable: opened food and drink (teas), opened bath and self-care products, and personalised gift message cards, for hygiene and safety reasons.
  • Signature boxes: if you return some items from a signature box, the refund is based on the discounted box price shared across its items.
  • Refunds: issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after we receive the item.
  • Gift recipients: can exchange items for store credit without the purchaser being told.

To start a return, contact us with your order reference and choose "Returns" as the topic.
