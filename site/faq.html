Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a gift set and a Build-Your-Own gift box?

A gift set is a ready-made trio of iconic perfumes chosen by our team, with one set for her and one for him. It costs 10% less than buying the same perfumes separately. A Build-Your-Own gift box lets you choose 3 to 8 perfumes from the women's and men's sections. You pay the perfume prices plus AED 15 for the gift box and wrapping.

Can I change the perfumes in a gift set?

Yes. Open the gift set and choose Customise this set. Its perfumes are copied into the gift box builder, where you can add, remove or swap anything. A customised set is priced as a Build-Your-Own gift box.

Why can't I add a perfume to my gift box?

Either the perfume is sold out, you already have 3 of that perfume, or your gift box already has 8 different perfumes. Stock is checked again when you place your order, so you will never be charged for something we don't have.

Can I include a gift message?

Yes. Add a message of up to 200 characters in the box builder or in your cart. We print it on a card placed inside the lid. Prices are never printed in the box.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Online card payment is coming soon. For now, orders are placed on the website without payment at checkout.

Do you deliver outside the UAE?

Not yet. We deliver to all seven emirates.

How do you use my personal information?

We use your name, contact details and address only to process your order and answer your messages. They are visible only to store administrators. Our website analytics do not use cookies and do not record your IP address or name.

How to order

  1. Choose a gift set or single perfumes from the shop, or open the gift box builder and add 3 to 8 perfumes.
  2. Add a gift message if you like, then add it to your cart.
  3. In your cart, check quantities and the total. Delivery is free over AED 200.
  4. Enter your name, contact details and delivery address, then place your order.
  5. You'll see your order reference straight away (for example MB-261122-7K3QD). Keep it for any questions.

Delivery

  • Abu Dhabi and Dubai: 1–2 working days.
  • Other emirates: 2–4 working days.
  • Fees: free on orders of AED 200 or more; AED 15 otherwise.
  • Orders placed after 2 pm or on Saturday are packed the next working day.
  • Our courier contacts you by phone before delivery. If you're not available, they try again the next working day.

Returns policy and service guarantee

We want every order to arrive in perfect condition.

  • 7-day returns: return any unopened perfume in its original sealed packaging within 7 days of delivery for a full refund of that item's price.
  • Damaged or wrong perfumes: tell us within 48 hours with a photo and we'll replace it free of charge, including delivery.
  • Not returnable: perfumes that have been opened or used, or whose cellophane seal is broken, and personalised gift message cards, for hygiene reasons.
  • Gift sets: if you return some perfumes from a gift set, the refund is based on the discounted set price shared across its perfumes.
  • Refunds: issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days after we receive the item.
  • Gift recipients: can exchange perfumes for store credit without the purchaser being told.

To start a return, contact us with your order reference and choose "Returns" as the topic.
